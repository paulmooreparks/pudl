Selection actions and margin notes

Select a passage to offer Quote. Resize the container to turn notes into markers. The article keeps its width and position.

A selection can carry several actions without giving the article a pressable surface. The application decides what each action does.

Margin notes stay beside their passages where room permits. In a narrow panel, numbered buttons lead to the same mounted notes below the article.
